# Guide: Changing Developer Name to "Hollowscan" on Apple App Store

This document outlines the professional path to changing the App Store "Seller" name from **Lee Smith** to **Hollowscan**.

## 1. The Core Requirement
Apple currently lists the account as an **Individual** membership. Individual accounts must strictly use the legal name of the person. To display a business name like "Hollowscan," the account must be converted to an **Organization** membership.

## 2. Prerequisites
Before contacting Apple, ensure you have the following in place:

*   **Legal Entity:** You must have a registered business entity (e.g., LLC, Corp, Ltd). Apple will not update the name to "Hollowscan" unless you can prove it is a registered legal entity or a registered DBA (Doing Business As).
*   **D-U-N-S Number:** This is a free, 9-digit identification number for your business. Apple uses it to verify the organization's existence.
    *   **Check/Apply here:** [Dun & Bradstreet](https://www.dnb.com/duns-number/get-a-duns.html)
    *   *Note: It typically takes 5–30 days to receive.*

## 3. Step-by-Step Transition Process

1.  **Initiate Request:**
    *   Visit the [Apple Developer Contact Us](https://developer.apple.com/contact/) page.
    *   Navigate to: **Membership and Account** > **Account Updates**.
    *   Message: *"I would like to convert my Individual membership to an Organization membership for my business: Hollowscan."*

2.  **Verification Call/Email:**
    *   An Apple representative will contact you via phone or email.
    *   They will verify that you are the founder/owner with authority to represent the business.
    *   You will be asked to provide your D-U-N-S number.

3.  **Legal Review:**
    *   Apple will review your business standing. Once approved, the account type will change.

4.  **Final Update:**
    *   Once the account is an "Organization," your "Seller Name" in the App Store will automatically update to your business name.
    *   You can set the "Public Seller Name" to just **Hollowscan** (omitting "LLC" or "Ltd") if you have a registered DBA for that shorter name.
